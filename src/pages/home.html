03 / GOOD TO KNOWTHE LITTLE DETAILS

Good
questions.

The essentials on counting beats, finding a song’s BPM, and getting the most out of Tap Tempo.

What is Tap Tempo?+

A tap tempo tool calculates beats per minute (BPM) from the time between repeated taps. Tap each beat of a song or metronome to estimate its tempo.

How many taps do I need?+

Two taps give an initial BPM estimate. Four or more steady taps produce a more reliable reading. This tool keeps a rolling history of recent intervals.

What does ½× or 2× mean?+

Some songs can be counted in half-time or double-time. These controls adjust the displayed BPM without changing the underlying tap timestamps.

Why does my BPM change as I tap?+

Human taps vary slightly. The counter takes the median of recent accepted intervals and ignores isolated outliers. Follow a consistent beat for the best estimate.

Can I use this on my phone?+

Yes. The large button works with touch. On desktop, you can press the spacebar repeatedly when you're not typing into a form.

Does this automatically analyze music files?+

No. This is a manual Tap Tempo tool. No files are uploaded or analyzed, and no account is needed.
