Good to know

Tap Tempo FAQ

Practical answers about measuring beats per minute.

What is Tap Tempo?

A tap tempo tool calculates beats per minute (BPM) from the time between repeated taps. Tap each beat of a song or metronome to estimate its tempo.

How many taps do I need?

Two taps provide an initial BPM estimate. Four or more steady taps make the reading more reliable. This tool keeps a rolling history of recent intervals.

What does ½× or 2× mean?

Some songs can be counted in half time or double time. These controls show the corresponding BPM without changing the underlying tap timestamps.

Why does my BPM change as I tap?

Human taps vary. The counter uses the median of recent accepted intervals and ignores isolated outliers. For the most accurate result, follow a steady, audible beat.

Can I use this on a phone?

Yes. The large tap button responds to touch or mouse clicks. On desktop, press Space repeatedly when you are not typing in a form field.

Does this tool analyze my music files?

No. This version measures manually tapped beats. It does not record, upload, or automatically analyze audio tracks.
