Understand the math

How to calculate BPM delay time

At 120 beats per minute, a quarter note lasts 500 milliseconds.

What does dotted delay mean?

A dotted note lasts 1.5 times the base note. At 120 BPM, a straight eighth note is 250 ms; a dotted eighth is 375 ms.

What is a triplet delay?

A triplet takes two-thirds of the corresponding straight note value. An eighth-note triplet at 120 BPM lasts approximately 166.7 ms.

Does this calculate acoustic RT60?

No. This tool provides tempo-synced musical timing. Physical reverberation times depend on room acoustics and cannot be calculated from BPM alone.

Can I paste these values into a DAW?

Yes. Use “Copy table” to copy note timings in milliseconds, then enter the appropriate value in your DAW's delay or effect controls.
