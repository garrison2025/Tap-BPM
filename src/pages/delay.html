04 / EXPLAINEDNO GUESSWORK

The timing
behind it.

At 120 beats per minute, a quarter note lasts 500 milliseconds.

What does dotted delay mean?+

A dotted note lasts 1.5 times the base note. At 120 BPM, a straight eighth is 250 ms and a dotted eighth is 375 ms.

What is a triplet delay?+

A triplet lasts two-thirds of the straight note. An eighth-note triplet at 120 BPM is approximately 166.7 ms.

Does this calculate room reverb (RT60)?+

No. It calculates tempo-synced musical timing. Actual room reverberation depends on acoustic factors, not BPM alone.

Can I paste these into my DAW?+

Yes. Click a timing cell to copy that exact displayed value in milliseconds, or copy the full table. Your selected meter changes bar references, not the quarter, eighth or triplet note durations.

How is a 6/8 bar calculated?+

At quarter-note BPM, one 6/8 measure contains six eighth notes, equal to three quarter notes. At 120 quarter-note BPM, one 6/8 bar is 1,500 ms. If the tempo is given in dotted-quarter beats per minute instead, multiply that BPM by 1.5 before entering it here.
