What these tools do

Use the Tap Tempo tool to estimate BPM from manual taps, or enter any BPM in the timing calculator to see note durations in milliseconds. Calculations run locally in your browser with no account and no audio uploads.

The goal is to make music timing utilities fast, accurate and accessible for producers, musicians, DJs and anyone learning rhythm.

How we calculate results

The Tap Tempo calculator measures time between accepted taps, computes a rolling median and excludes isolated intervals that deviate significantly from the median. Timing starts with your second valid tap. The delay calculator uses the formula 60,000 / BPM for quarter-note milliseconds.

What this site does not do

It does not identify BPM automatically from songs, provide medical hearing tests, or calculate physical room reverberation time. {{ABOUT_DATA_STATUS}}
