Plain-language privacy notice

Your tempo. Your device.

The music timing tools run in your browser without an account, upload or paid API.

Data processed by the tools

Tap times and BPM calculations are handled in browser memory. The last measured BPM may be stored in browser sessionStorage to transfer it to the delay calculator while you navigate in the same tab/session. Tap timestamps stay in your browser, but a selected BPM can appear in a shared URL, as described below.

A shareable link may contain a BPM query parameter such as ?bpm=120. When that link is opened, its URL may be included in routine hosting request logs or browser history. The link contains no tap timestamps or audio files.
