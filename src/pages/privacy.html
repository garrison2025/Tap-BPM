Plain-language privacy notice

Your tempo. Your device.

The current prototype is designed to operate entirely in your browser without an account, upload or paid API.

Data processed by the tools

Tap times and BPM calculations are handled in browser memory. The last measured BPM may be stored in browser sessionStorage to transfer it to the delay calculator while you navigate in the same tab/session. The prototype does not send that value to a server.

The BPM calculator can also read a BPM value from the URL query parameter (?bpm=120) for sharing. Do not place sensitive information in such links.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This prototype does not include analytics trackers, advertising scripts, accounts or third-party services. If ads or analytics are activated for a publicly released site, this notice must be revised first and the required consent mechanisms provided for relevant regions.

Hosting and network logs

When published using a hosting provider, ordinary HTTP requests for the website files may be logged or processed by that provider. That operational processing is separate from the local BPM calculation itself. The final host, controller contact and retention practices must be specified before public launch.

Contact

See the contact and feedback page. In the prototype, an inbox has not yet been configured.
